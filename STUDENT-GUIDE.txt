THE MIDNIGHT EDITION
Student field guide | North Quay Press

An overnight print job was late. Two transfers reached different destinations.
Your job is to explain the delay, decide which transfer fits the approved job,
and leave the morning analyst enough evidence to continue the inquiry.

Allow 75–95 minutes for the eight stations, practice clippings and final written
briefing. The clock times are the times in the story. You can pause at any point.

GET STARTED
1. Open index.html or the link your instructor gave you.
2. Click Open shift file. On a later visit, Resume shift opens the first
   unfinished station. Your work is saved on this browser when storage is available.
3. Read At your desk. Open Worked example or Field guide for beginners when a
   term is unfamiliar. Then read Your assignment.
4. Click a record headline to unfold its original fields. The time above the
   source text is when the collector received it. The source text shows the
   original event time. Click the headline again to fold it.
5. Search source archive opens the full case in a read-only drawer. Choose a
   generating source or enter text such as a user, IP or record ID. Open a
   matching slip to read it. Close archive to return to your assignment.
6. Each station has a main assignment and two small practice clippings.
   The clippings are independent examples. Answer each short question from
   the evidence printed directly above it.
7. Click Check station. If you need to revise an answer, read the feedback,
   open the appropriate hint, change the answer and check again. Retries are free.
8. After a pass, click Next station. The Shift file index lets you revisit signed
   stations. Pause motion switches off decorative movement and shortens replay.

STATION 1 | TAKE THE CALL | about 8 minutes
Open HELP to read the operator’s complaint. Open ALERT to find the SIEM’s
grouping field. Compare QUEUE with HELP. Choose the security scope, review
window and evidence handling method. Click Seal original records. Download
sealed JSON if you want a local copy. Answer the two practice clippings.
Discuss why Press Operations and the incident analyst need different records.

STATION 2 | ROUTE THE RECORDS | about 10 minutes
Open each record in turn. Find the field that names the action or result.
Under that slip, choose the question this source can answer. Read the tray
label to see where you filed it. Complete all five routes. In the clippings,
read the summary count and identify the upstream host. Explain why the
number of SIEM rows can differ from the number of observed failures.

STATION 3 | REPAIR THE CLOCK | about 12 minutes
Open CLOCK and VPN. Choose the offset to subtract from the gateway’s local
clock. Read the conversion preview. Click Play arrival reel and let it finish.
Look at the SIEM arrival order. Now click the event strips in the order the
activity actually happened. Your cut appears in the dark strip area. Use Undo
last strip or Clear reel to correct it. For the practice clippings, check the
calendar day as well as the hour. Compare two UTC timestamps to measure delay.

STATION 4 | TRACE THE SESSION | about 10 minutes
Read AUTH, GUEST and VPN. Look for repeated and conflicting values. Follow the
VPN’s assigned address into FILE and a proxy record. Choose a link on each
row. Read the comparison underneath, then open the original to verify it.
Decide how much weight to put on the automatic alert. Use the practice records
to distinguish a shared public address from an internal address reused later.

STATION 5 | TEST THE APPROVAL | about 12 minutes
Open JOB. Read its source, destination and UTC window. Switch on ONE condition
and click Run ticket model. Look at the five rows. The first two are observed
flows. The three test inputs show what your rule would do in other situations.
Add conditions and rerun. The results return to Awaiting run whenever you
change a switch. Compare all rows against the ticket, choose the actual flow
needing review, and solve the practice clippings. Be ready to explain one
mistake that a broad rule would have made.

STATION 6 | REDLINE THE AI DRAFT | about 10 minutes
Read one draft line. Open the relevant source slip above the editor. Choose
replacement wording and cite the record containing the verifying field.
The original line is crossed out and your edit appears immediately. Do this
for all three lines. In the clippings, check transfer direction, units and the
exact operation. If a draft sounds certain, find the field that supports it.

STATION 7 | REQUEST A FOLLOW-UP | about 8 minutes
Read what each correspondent could check. Click Send simulated enquiry on
the relevant cards. All replies are local fictional training material.
Read the replies before choosing what was learned and what is still unknown.
Complete both clippings. Explain which enquiry helps operations continue
while the analyst investigates the unresolved transfer.

STATION 8 | SIGN THE MORNING HAND-OFF | about 12 minutes
Open the relevant originals. Select the four citations requested. Build the
confirmed observation, interpretation, unresolved question and next action.
Write a short follow-up in your own words, naming what should be checked
and why. The browser checks that the note is present and sufficiently long.
Your instructor assesses its reasoning. Click Preview hand-off and read it
aloud. After any edit to the report, preview it again before signing.
Complete the final practice pair and click Sign the hand-off.

DOWNLOADS
Click View signed edition, enter the name for your certificate, and download
the PNG. Download case report (TXT) saves the signed hand-off with your
citations and written follow-up. If you revise the final station later,
preview it and sign it again to update the saved report.

WHEN SOMETHING FEELS TRICKY
Start with the question above the evidence. Circle the specific field you
need. Use the worked example, station hint and clipping hint. The correct
relationship usually needs more than a nearby time or one shared address.
Keep the browser and site address the same to resume progress.
